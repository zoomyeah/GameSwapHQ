For Developers

Want your game on GameSwapHQ? GameSwapHQ is a curated gaming portal, so every submitted game is reviewed before it is added.

Game Requirements

  • Games must be clean, kid-friendly, and appropriate for a general audience.
  • Games should be complete, playable, and function properly.
  • Games should be reasonably polished and provide a good experience for players.
  • Games must not contain inappropriate, offensive, or disturbing content.
  • Games must not contain unapproved external links or unsafe websites.
  • Games should use original content or content that you have permission to use.

Quality & Safety

Every game is manually reviewed before it is published on GameSwapHQ. Games may be tested for functionality, quality, safety, and appropriate content. A submission is not guaranteed to be accepted.

What Happens After You Submit?

Your game will be reviewed manually. If it meets the GameSwapHQ requirements and passes our review, it may be added to the website. If changes are needed, you may be contacted before the game is published.

Submission Guidelines

Please submit a game that you created and provide accurate information about the game and its content. Do not submit someone else's project without permission.
